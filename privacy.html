NutriSync

Privacy policy

Last updated: 13 October 2026

NutriSync is made by Scott Milne, an individual developer based in New Zealand ("I", "me"). This policy explains what the app does with your information. The short version: your data goes from your iPhone straight to your own Huawei Health account. I don't run a server that stores it, and I never sell it or use it for advertising.

What NutriSync reads

With your permission, NutriSync reads nutrition data from Apple Health on your iPhone. This includes dietary energy (kilojoules or calories), protein, fat, carbohydrate, fibre, sugar, sodium and water, along with the time each entry was logged. It does not read any other Apple Health data.

What NutriSync sends to Huawei

When you connect your Huawei ID, NutriSync uploads those nutrition entries to your Huawei Health account using Huawei Health Kit. It may also read nutrition records from your Huawei Health account for the last 7 days, only to avoid creating duplicates and to apply edits you make to a meal. Huawei's handling of your data is covered by Huawei's privacy statement.

What is stored, and where

  • On your iPhone: your Huawei sign-in tokens (in the iOS Keychain), your sync settings, and a record of which entries have been synced.
  • On a NutriSync server: no health data is stored. If a sign-in helper service is used to exchange Huawei sign-in codes, it handles the sign-in code only, passes it straight back to your phone and keeps no copy.

What NutriSync does not do

  • It does not sell, rent or share your data with anyone other than Huawei, at your request.
  • It does not use your data for advertising or marketing, or to build a profile of you.
  • It does not include advertising or third-party analytics.
  • It does not give medical advice.

Data obtained from Apple Health is used only to provide the sync you asked for, as Apple's rules require.

Your choices

  • You can turn off NutriSync's access to Apple Health at any time in the iPhone Settings or Health app.
  • You can disconnect your Huawei account in the app, which deletes the sign-in tokens from your phone. You can also revoke access in your Huawei ID account settings.
  • Deleting the app removes everything it stored on your phone.
  • Data already synced to Huawei Health stays in your Huawei account until you delete it there.

Children

NutriSync is not intended for children under 16.

Your rights

Under the New Zealand Privacy Act 2020 you can ask to access or correct personal information about you. Because I don't hold your health data, most requests are best handled in Apple Health or Huawei Health, but please get in touch if you have questions.

Changes

If this policy changes, I will update this page and the date at the top.
